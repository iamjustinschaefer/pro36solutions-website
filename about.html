About

Built by trainers, for trainers

We run a dog-training facility ourselves, and we wrote Pro 36 from the inside, one workflow at a time.

Why this exists

We got tired of stitching tools together

Running a dog-training business means juggling leads, consults, enrollments, daily training notes, client updates, and billing. Most kennel management software only covers reservations and boarding — the training program itself gets left out.

So we built the tool we wished existed: one system that follows a dog's whole journey from first inquiry through graduation, with program and curriculum tracking at the center of it.

See it in action at our own facility, Pro 36 Canine Academy — we run every part of our own program on this platform.
