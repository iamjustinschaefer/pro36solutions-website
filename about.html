Why this exists

We got tired of stitching tools together

Running a board-and-train facility means juggling leads, consults, enrollments, daily training notes, client updates, and billing — and most software built for kennels only really covers reservations and boarding, not the actual training program.

So we built the tool we wished existed: one system that follows a dog's whole journey, from the first inquiry through graduation, with the program and curriculum tracking at the center instead of bolted on as an afterthought.
