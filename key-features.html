Key features

Everything from first lead to next month's invoice

Six pieces of running a training business, tracked in one connected system, from the first phone call to the invoice you send at graduation.

01 — Leads & consults

A pipeline that doesn't leak

Every inquiry gets tracked from first contact through consult to enrollment, so no lead just quietly disappears in a group text or a sticky note.

  • Lead status tracked from inquiry to consult to enrollment
  • Nothing sits untouched without you noticing
  • Consult notes tied to the dog, visible later
02 — Enrollments

One record, start to finish

Client, dog, and program tied together the moment someone enrolls — so training logs, updates, and billing are all working off the same source of truth.

  • Program, client, and dog linked automatically
  • No re-entering the same info across separate tools
03 — Training logs & program checklists

Fully customizable programs

Adjust the software to match your specific program, step by step through your exact curriculum. Structured tracking any trainer on staff can pick up and continue, whether they logged day one or day twenty.

  • Checklists built around your exact program
  • Daily logs any staff trainer can read and continue
  • A running history tied to the dog, so it never leaves when a trainer does
04 — Voice notes

Talk it, don't type it

Out in the field with a leash in one hand, you're not stopping to type. Just talk — your voice note is transcribed straight into the dog's training log, no typing required.

  • Hands-free, on the spot — no phone typing between reps
  • Transcribed automatically into the dog's record
  • Feeds straight into training logs and owner updates
05 — Weekly owner updates

AI drafts it, you send it

Updates and training recommendations are generated automatically from the same training logs your staff is already keeping, and can be sent directly from the platform — no separate email client, no starting from a blank page.

  • Built from real training-log data, written in your voice
  • AI-generated training recommendations included
  • Sent straight from the platform, ready to review first
06 — Billing & payments

Track billing, and get paid online

Billing stays tracked against the enrollment record you already created. Connect your own Stripe account and send a client a real, payable link straight from an outstanding charge — they pay online, and it's marked paid automatically. No separate invoicing tool, no manual reconciliation at the end of the month.

  • Payments tracked directly against enrollments
  • Send clients a real, payable link — paid automatically once they pay
  • Money goes straight to your own bank account — we never touch or hold it
  • No separate books to reconcile
